PhD candidate at UCLA, graduating June 2027

Building multimodal models that are capable, efficient, robust, and fast.

I’m Wenhan, a computer science PhD student at UCLA advised by Baharan Mirzasoleiman. I work across the life of a multimodal foundation model: curating the data it learns from, post-training it with SFT, RL, and distillation, defending it against poisoning and backdoor attacks, and scaling its training and test-time inference at lower compute and latency. Lately that has extended to agentic systems, where I train lightweight orchestrators that decompose long-horizon tasks, delegate to sub-agents, and extend the effective execution horizon at a fraction of frontier-model cost.

      V2T: Distilling visual reasoning into text space

      Cisco Research, 2025

      Models that “think with images” are expensive at inference. V2T distills interleaved visual–textual chain-of-thought into a 7B text-only student, using logit distillation, attention alignment, and a GRPO stage rewarded for correctness and visual grounding. Training runs up to 42× faster than state-of-the-art baselines.

    2. Early
      reasoning quality as a signal for data curation

      Reasoning quality emerges early: Data curation for reasoning models

      ICML 2026

      Reasoning quality emerges early enough to guide which data a reasoning model should learn from. This work turns that early signal into a data-curation strategy for reasoning-model training.

      Project pagearXivCode
    3. 0%
      targeted poisoning attack success, down from 93.75%

      SafeCLIP: Pre-training CLIP against targeted data poisoning and backdoor attacks

      ICML 2024

      Poisoning a tiny fraction of CLIP’s web-scale data is enough to hijack it. SafeCLIP splits training data into safe and risky sets and grows the safe set over time, breaking both targeted poisoning and backdoor attacks without hurting clean performance.

      Project pagearXivCode
    4. 2.60×
      lossless decoding speedup across 10 multimodal benchmarks, against 2.19× for EAGLE-3

      HAWK: Rethinking multimodal drafting for speculative decoding

      Samsung Research America, 2026

      A target-aware drafter for vision-language models. HAWK grounds its drafts in the target’s compressed visual states and selected hidden layers, and a draft-aware training loss keeps it aligned with the target over multi-step drafting. Average acceptance length goes up 23%.

      arXiv
